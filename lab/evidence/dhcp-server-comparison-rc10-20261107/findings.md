# ASTRA rc10 DHCP server comparison — complete

**Verdict:** dnsmasq improved recovery but did not deliver prompt usable access after CoA. With a one-hour lease, fresh TCP to the new segment recovered automatically at T2 after **51m52.573s** for early corp→quarantine and **22m49.615s** for near-T1 quarantine→corp. No endpoint renewal or reconnect was performed during either primary run. The BusyBox udhcpd baseline remains the published [one-hour Linux result](https://github.com/KidCarmi/nac/blob/a6df7627bcdf87d9e4b3c81d412dc91a949c4bd8/lab/evidence/linux-lease-rc10-20261107/findings.md).

## Fixed components

NAC rc10 commit `b39649f138e126ed6689a53b590855061042e341`, nacd SHA-256 `35699eac162cc2a7315efced101bee3ec18ed87ab1cb108f06f9bb1a7b48195e`, FreeRADIUS 3.2.5, vEOS-lab 4.29.2F, same EVE topology, same one-NIC Ubuntu 22.04.5 systemd-networkd endpoint and EAP-TLS certificate. Corp and quarantine HTTP targets each returned 200 from their own segment, and EVE forwarding remained 0.

The only intended treatment was replacing BusyBox udhcpd with Ubuntu dnsmasq-base 2.91-0ubuntu0.22.04.2 on both original service guests. Both dnsmasq configs used `dhcp-authoritative`, their original subnet/route/DNS settings and a 3,600-second lease. Package SHA-256: `c999f2c92633d753edcdd67fb039f5866e9413500b2c764993cbb6bb26393309`. After the run, the original 120-second udhcpd services were restored and dnsmasq stopped; the endpoint supplicant and captures were stopped and the switch session cleared.

## Preflight, separate from the endpoint result

In disposable Linux namespaces, a crafted broadcast DHCPREQUEST with an old-subnet `ciaddr=10.70.120.80` reached dnsmasq's interface but received no reply in four seconds. A same-subnet control DHCPREQUEST generated by the same script got DHCPACK. See `preflight-packets.pcap`, `preflight-dnsmasq.log`, `preflight-dnsmasq.conf` and `qualification/dhcp_rebind_preflight.py`. This preflight does not establish what the real endpoint will do.

## Early corp to quarantine, continuous primary observation

Corp DHCPACK was 14:57:11.749 UTC, address 10.70.10.80/24, option 51=3600, T1=1800, T2=3150, corp route and DNS 10.70.10.10. The product Quarantine CoA was requested 14:57:53.316828094 UTC, about 42 seconds after ACK. vEOS reported EAPOL SUCCESS VLAN20 and the NAC rule remained `bound-eap-corp` under policy v4 with an operator quarantine override. Fresh corp TCP was first denied +1.385 seconds; quarantine TCP had not succeeded through 15:23:19 UTC (25m26s). The endpoint still had the corp lease, route and DNS. No endpoint renewal or reconnect was made.

The endpoint saved T1/T2/lifetime as advertised. A DHCP pcap snapshot through 15:33 UTC showed no post-T1 DHCP packet. A bounded ARP capture started at 15:33 shows unanswered requests for the old corp service/server IP. This does not prove which process triggered each ARP.

At **15:49:42.670 UTC**, about two seconds after advertised T2, the real endpoint broadcast DHCPREQUEST with old `ciaddr=10.70.10.80` and source IP 0.0.0.0. The quarantine dnsmasq server sent **DHCPNAK** at 15:49:42.675. The endpoint sent Discover at 15:49:42.688, received Offer for 10.70.20.52 at 15:49:45.740 and ACK at 15:49:45.750; options 51/58/59 were 3600/1800/3150. It changed address, route and DNS without manual action. First fresh quarantine TCP succeeded at **15:49:45.890**, **+3,112.573 s (51m52.573s)** after the NAC Quarantine request. Corp remained blocked. This is an automatic recovery PASS for the measured direction, with a long application outage.

The synthetic preflight did not reproduce the real rebind packet's source address: it used the old-subnet IP, whereas systemd-networkd transmitted from 0.0.0.0 at T2. Its no-response result cannot be used to predict the real endpoint result. The actual endpoint packet capture is authoritative.

## Near-T1 quarantine to corp, continuous primary observation

The quarantine lease ACK was 15:49:45.750 UTC with address 10.70.20.52 and options 51/58/59=3600/1800/3150. The pre-action endpoint probe showed fresh quarantine TCP success and corp denial. A one-shot timer invoked the documented rc10 Restore API at **16:19:27.455340398 UTC**, about 18 seconds before advertised T1. RADIUS CoA requested VLAN10 at 16:19:28.269, received CoA-ACK at 16:19:28.272, and returned Access-Accept VLAN10 at 16:19:28.548; EAPOL Success followed at 16:19:28.551. The switch reported VLAN10. NAC policy v4 matched `bound-eap-corp` without an operator override. Fresh quarantine TCP first failed at **16:19:29.142**, **+1.687 s**. The endpoint kept its quarantine address, route and DNS, so corp was not reachable.

At **16:19:46.225 UTC**, the endpoint sent a unicast DHCPREQUEST from 10.70.20.52 to old server 10.70.20.10, without a response. At **16:42:14.871 UTC**, approximately T2, it broadcast DHCPREQUEST with source 0.0.0.0 and old `ciaddr=10.70.20.52`. Corp dnsmasq sent DHCPNAK at 16:42:14.876. Discover followed at 16:42:14.877, Offer for 10.70.10.52 at 16:42:17.925 and ACK at 16:42:17.949. The endpoint changed address, route and DNS without manual action. First fresh corp TCP succeeded at **16:42:17.070**, **+1,369.615 s (22m49.615s)** after Restore. Quarantine remained blocked. Endpoint and EVE clocks differ by up to about one second; do not infer subsecond ordering between the probe timestamp and EVE packet timestamps. The fresh TCP probes have 0.8-second per-target timeout.

Compared with the published BusyBox near-T1 Restore (+1,811.866 s), dnsmasq reached corp **442.251 s (7m22.251s)** sooner. The BusyBox early Quarantine observation was manually interrupted at 148 seconds, so no full-duration early-direction recovery comparison is valid.

The [early probe](early/endpoint-probe.jsonl), [near-T1 probe](near/endpoint-probe.jsonl), [early endpoint pcap](early/endpoint.pcap0) and [near-T1 endpoint pcap](near/endpoint.pcap0) are complete bounded records. [Early timing](analysis-early-final.txt) and [near-T1 timing](analysis-near-final.txt) provide the probe calculations. [Service health](service-health-final.json) verifies HTTP 200 independently from both segments and `ip_forward=0`. [Manifest](sha256-manifest.json), [topology](NAC-Pilot-DHCP.unl), and [rerun instructions](rerun.md) complete the package. This is a Linux systemd-networkd and virtual vEOS result. It does not establish behavior for Windows 10/11 or a physical switch.

Interpret the table by lease age and direction. The baseline early Quarantine was interrupted by a diagnostic at 148 seconds and must stay labelled incomplete beyond that point. It is not a full one-hour baseline for that direction. A NAK first received at T2 could still leave a long outage; measure the actual time before recommending any DHCP server change.
