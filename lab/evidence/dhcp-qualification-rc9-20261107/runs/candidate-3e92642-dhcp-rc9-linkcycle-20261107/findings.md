# pilot-rc9 endpoint stop and explicit port link-down — mixed result

Exact commit `3e92642110f97d3f42a43652d33479e82cfbcc35`, nacd SHA-256 `d2d93e3ddc40a7eadcddfd3275f3d8a7aa5610ca07b43eb219bb9d59267a3749`; isolated EVE topology unchanged and all other labs stopped. Initial [NAC session](before-session.txt) was `2a94923e-b07e-40e2-ad3f-36f28cafcb79`, policy v2 corp, Et1 SUCCESS VLAN10. The [helper](endpoint-stopped.txt) stopped only disposable endpoint EVE node 32; switch node31, NAC node35 and segment services stayed running.

**Virtual unplug simulation — BLOCKED by vEOS/EVE link behavior.** Expected a stopped endpoint VM to withdraw link on Et1 and end the session. Actual: after EVE reported node32 stopped, [switch readback](runner.log) still showed Et1 EAPOL SUCCESS VLAN10 and NAC [continued to label the old session connected](after-stop-session.txt) from a fresh `show dot1x hosts` observation. Thus VM stop did not simulate a physical cable unplug in this virtual topology; the product had no link-down signal to observe. [Endpoint restart](endpoint-started.txt) brought node32 back, but its valid supplicant did not auto-start. Its old corp DHCP address returned while the switch still held the old authorization; this is a lab/virtual switch limitation, not proof that the product missed an actual link-down.

**Explicit switch port link-down — PASS, API/packet; operator-triggered.** With the endpoint node running, ASTRA used the isolated switch's [Ethernet1 shutdown, two-second pause and no shutdown](operator-port-bounce.txt). This was an operator action and must not be counted as automatic reconnect. RADIUS [Accounting Stop](radius-timeline.txt) for old Class `nac:afb4aadb-595d-40fa-b88c-1c1e6c839cea` arrived at **02:43:19.324Z**; the product [accounting events](accounting-events.txt) associated the Stop with old session `2a94923e-…`. [NAC readback](after-bounce-session.txt) had **zero active sessions**, the switch had no dot1x host, and an [endpoint probe](runner.log) found both TCP targets blocked despite a retained corp DHCP lease.

ASTRA then **explicitly started** the valid `wpa_supplicant` at 02:44:16Z. RADIUS Access-Accept VLAN10 at **02:44:20.419Z** and Accounting Start at .422Z led to [new session](reconnected-session.txt) `fc693de4-0ada-4389-a241-dc1c74d04d19`, new decision `059fd201-42d6-4758-b74f-9fce194dd245`, policy v2 corp, fresh eAPI SUCCESS VLAN10. The switch showed Et1 SUCCESS VLAN10; endpoint corp DHCP 10.70.10.80/24 and TCP were usable, quarantine blocked. The captured [endpoint EAPOL](endpoint.pcap), [RADIUS](radius.pcap) and [timeline](radius-timeline.txt) distinguish the two sessions. Four captures had zero known plaintext credential matches [scan](credential-scan.txt).

An authorized physical Arista switch with an actual cable disconnect/reconnect is needed to qualify physical unplug behavior. This test does not claim the Linux supplicant recovered automatically after the endpoint VM restart; it was started manually and that action is in the evidence.
