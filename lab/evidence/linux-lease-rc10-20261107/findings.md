# Linux one-hour DHCP comparison — complete

Candidate and topology are pinned in `plan.txt`; exact live versions are in `versions.txt` and `switch-inventory.txt`. This Linux result is separate from the Windows one-hour PASS and the earlier Linux 120-second baseline.

## Early corp to quarantine

**Expected:** valid EAP-TLS is admitted to corp, then a NAC Quarantine CoA enforces VLAN20 and the one-NIC DHCP endpoint obtains usable quarantine addressing without intervention. **Actual through the 148-second natural observation:** NAC recorded Quarantine at 12:13:00.5106218 UTC, switch CoA was acknowledged, RADIUS reauthorized VLAN20, vEOS reported EAPOL SUCCESS VLAN20, and the corp service remained independently healthy. Fresh corp TCP was first denied at +2.025 s. The endpoint still had 10.70.10.80/24 with corp DNS and route, and no quarantine TCP succeeded by 12:15:28.366 UTC. Carrier remained up. `analysis-primary.txt` and the bounded probe establish this lower bound; do not label it automatic recovery.

**Separate diagnosis:** `networkctl renew ens3` at 12:15:28.520 UTC did not change the address or produce quarantine reachability through 12:16:33.679 UTC (`analysis-manual-renew.txt`). `networkctl down ens3; networkctl up ens3` began 12:16:33.817 UTC and obtained 10.70.20.80/24 with quarantine DNS/route by 12:16:39.922 UTC. Fresh quarantine TCP succeeded +6.911 s after that manual action, with corp blocked (`analysis-manual-reconnect.txt`). The DHCP capture shows a NACK for a request for the old corp address, then Discover/Offer/Request/ACK in quarantine. This isolates stale endpoint addressing; it is not an automatic PASS.

## Near-T1 quarantine to corp — automatic only at lease expiry

The quarantine DHCP ACK was 12:16:40.067577 UTC, lease option 51=3600 seconds and no options 58/59. The derived T1/T2 were approximately 12:46:40 and 13:09:10 UTC; expiry was 13:16:40 UTC. Product Restore occurred at **12:46:28.165764 UTC**, about 12 seconds before T1. Quarantine TCP was first denied **+1.419 s**; the switch showed EAPOL SUCCESS VLAN10. At 12:46:40.716 the endpoint sent a unicast DHCP Request from its old 10.70.20.80 address to the now inaccessible quarantine server 10.70.20.10, without a reply. At T2, 13:09:09.809, it broadcast a Request carrying the old quarantine `ciaddr`; the corp bridge captured it at 13:09:09.811, but the server did not respond. Further such broadcasts at 13:12:54.972, 13:14:47.554 and 13:15:47.565 also went unanswered.

At **13:16:40.126 UTC**, lease expiry triggered a *new DHCP Discover*. The corp server offered 10.70.10.80 and ACKed it at 13:16:40.134, with another 3600-second lease. The timestamp-sorted overlap of the original and separately identified 360-second extension probes contains **6,735 rows**. It shows corp address, route and DNS at 13:16:40.013 on the endpoint clock, then the first successful fresh corp TCP at **13:16:40.032**, **+1,811.866 s (30m11.866s)** after Restore. There were 204 subsequent successful corp samples and no successful quarantine sample after Restore, through 13:20:18.688. The 0.1-second ordering discrepancy between the endpoint and EVE packet clocks is clock skew; the subsecond packet-to-TCP order should not be inferred from those cross-host timestamps. Carrier remained up and there was no manual endpoint action during this Restore. The packet and probe evidence support **PASS for eventual automatic recovery at lease expiry**, with a roughly half-hour application outage after Restore. This is not a claim of prompt recovery. Both services independently returned HTTP 200; EVE forwarding was 0 and the switch still showed EAPOL SUCCESS VLAN10.

The primary probe ended before lease expiry, so the overlapping extension probe was necessary. Its separate run ID is `linux1h-expiry-20261107`; the merged file is `endpoint-probe-combined.jsonl`. `analysis-near-final.txt`, `dhcp-timeline.txt` and the four bounded pcaps contain the final correlation. Early corp→quarantine showed **no automatic recovery within the 148-second observation window**. The longer-term automatic result for that direction is **BLOCKED by the subsequent diagnostic intervention**, which is labelled separately; it cannot be called a permanent failure or an eventual pass. The near-T1 Restore is the uninterrupted one-hour result.
